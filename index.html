Story

Introduction

Set ten years after the death of the notorious crime lord Geese Howard, the city of South Town has become more peaceful. Now, the stage is in the neighboring city of Second South Town. A new fighting tournament called "King of Fighters: Maximum Mayhem" starts in the area, and several characters related with the fighters from the old era of King of Fighters tournaments participate within it.

Features

T.O.P. System

The Tactical Offensive Position System or T.O.P., is a new system. Before each match begins, the player is given option to adjust the T.O.P. meter's influence onto three different places on their character's life meter (beginning, middle, end). When a character's life meter reaches the white T.O.P. section, the character will flash with their HP bar's T.O.P. section turning orange, and enter a special state which will grant them the following advantages : Increased damage input, slow health regeneration and ability to perform T.O.P. attacks, which often deal heavy amount of reduction to the defender's guard meter if blocked

Just Defense System

The Just Defense allows players to benefit from defending by activating it against incoming attacks at the last second within 7 frames. Players can perform Just Defense against any type of attack, even being able to Just Defend in midair despite the lack of air blocking. Just Defense has following advantages : Small health recovery and Power gauge gain for each successful Just Defense activation. Ability to Guard Cancel. Guard durability does not decrease unlike when blocking attacks normally. Less blockstun frames by 2 frames. And multiple successive hits can be blocked from continuous Just Defends rapidly like so, and it is possible to heal back into T.O.P. range and reactivate it once more

Breaking System

The Breaking is an advanced technique for interrupting and countering your opponent's special attacks. To successfully perform a “Breaking”, the player must anticipate the special attack and perform a precise command at the moment the attack hits. If the “Breaking” is successful, the attack is cancelled and the player can counter-attack. Breaking adds a strategic dimension to combat, encouraging analysis and anticipation. Mastering “Breaking” is essential for punishing abuse of special attacks and creating combo opportunities. However, it is a risky technique that can leave the player vulnerable if it fails.
